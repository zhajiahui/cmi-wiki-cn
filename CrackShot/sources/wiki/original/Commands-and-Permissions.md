## **░ Aliases**
    /shot, /cra, /cs

## **░ Commands**
    /shot                                   - Displays information on the plugin.
    /shot list all                          - Lists all weapons, excluding the ones that are hidden.
    /shot list <page>                       - Lists weapons by page number, excluding the ones that are hidden.
    /shot config reload                     - Reloads the configuration.
    /shot give <user> <weapon> <amount>     - Gives another player a weapon. You may specify an amount.
    /shot get <weapon> <amount>             - Gives the player a weapon. You may specify an amount.
    /shot reload                            - Reloads the weapon in hand.

## **░ Permissions**
By default, users can craft, buy and use all weapons.

    crackshot.reloadplugin      - Permission to reload the plugin.
    crackshot.list              - Permission to list weapons.
    crackshot.give.all          - Permission to give any weapon to players.
    crackshot.get.all           - Permission to get any weapon.
    crackshot.use.all           - Permission to use any weapon. (Default)
    crackshot.craft.all         - Permission to craft any weapon. (Default)
    crackshot.buy.all           - Permission to buy any weapon. (Default)
    crackshot.shops.all         - Permission to create and destroy stores for any weapon.
    crackshot.bypass.all        - Permission to bypass region-checks for any weapon.

    crackshot.give.<weapon>     - Permission to give a specific weapon to a player.
    crackshot.get.<weapon>      - Permission to get a specific weapon.
    crackshot.use.<weapon>      - Permission to use a specific weapon.
    crackshot.craft.<weapon>    - Permission to craft a specific weapon.
    crackshot.buy.<weapon>      - Permission to craft a specific weapon.
    crackshot.shops.<weapon>    - Permission to create and destroy stores for a specific weapon.
    crackshot.bypass.<weapon>   - Permission to bypass region-checks for a specific weapon.
