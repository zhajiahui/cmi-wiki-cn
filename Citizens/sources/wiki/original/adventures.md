## Dialog

Adventures uses a modified form of the Valve dialog system outlined in [this presentation](http://www.valvesoftware.com/publications/2012/GDC2012_Ruskin_Elan_DynamicDialog.pdf). This allows very dynamic and extensible dialog to take place.
