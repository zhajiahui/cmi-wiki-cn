## Old Info

The Citizens team has a IRC chat room on esper.net. You can join this room to receive support or make suggestions.

[Here](http://webchat.esper.net/?channels=citizens) is a web-based link to the chat, or point your favorite IRC client to #citizens on irc.esper.net

The rules for chatting on our IRC room, #citizens, are listed below:

1. No spamming, advertising, or soliciting.
2. Please don't ping the OPs unnecessarily, they are likely coding the next great feature. Don't worry, they check in regularly!
3. Don't ask to ask, just ask any question you may have.
4. Read through [this page](http://workaround.org/getting-help-on-irc) before chatting. It has lots of good tips.
5. Stick around when asking a question, we're busy people but will get to you as soon as possible.
