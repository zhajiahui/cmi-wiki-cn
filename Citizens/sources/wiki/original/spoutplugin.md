All plugins on this page require Citizens and Spout.

## Custom Skins

There are several extension plugins that allow the changing of Citizen/Spout NPC skins. For more info on Citizens Skins see [Skins](https://wiki.citizensnpcs.co/Skins "Skins")

- [[SimpleSkins Reloaded](http://dev.bukkit.org/server-mods/simpleskins-reloaded/)] by DreTax (CURRENT!)
- [[SkinTheCitizens2](http://forums.spout.org/threads/fun-misc-rpg-skinthecitizens2-customize-your-citizen2-npcs-sp-1298-cb-1-3-1-r2-1.4148/)] by tenowg (May be outdated)
- [[CitizensSkins](http://dev.bukkit.org/server-mods/citizenskins/)] by LargoUsagi (May be outdated)
- [[CitiSkins](http://forums.bukkit.org/threads/citiskins-skin-citizen-npcs.61273/)] by WindWaker (not released?)
