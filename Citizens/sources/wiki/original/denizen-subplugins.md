Denizen supports a variety of sub-plugins and links to other plugins

---

Depenizen: <https://github.com/DenizenScript/Depenizen> - adds support for a variety of external plugins

dIRCBot: <https://github.com/DenizenScript/dIRCBot> - adds a working IRCBot for Denizen scripts to control

dDiscordBot: <https://github.com/DenizenScript/dDiscordBot> - adds a working Discord bot for scripts to control

Webizen: <https://github.com/DenizenScript/Webizen> - adds the ability to host a website from your minecraft server
