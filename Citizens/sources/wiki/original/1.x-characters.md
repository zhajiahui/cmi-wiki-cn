Having a basic NPC is cool, but you may want it to utilize one of our character types.

To change an NPC's type, use the command /toggle [type]. Replace [type] with the NPC type that you want to toggle your NPC to become.

```
/toggle healer
```

## Character JARs

It is imperative that you have the NPC type's .jar file in your plugins/Citizens/types directory. If not, you won't be able to toggle an NPC to become that type. When updating Citizens, you must update your character types as well.

## Character Types

Toggleable Types (These types can be toggled):

- [1.x/Blacksmiths](https://wiki.citizensnpcs.co/1.x/Blacksmith "1.x/Blacksmith") can repair items, for a cost.
- [1.x/Guards](https://wiki.citizensnpcs.co/1.x/Guard "1.x/Guard") be bouncers, soldiers, or bodyguards.
- [1.x/Healers](https://wiki.citizensnpcs.co/1.x/Healer "1.x/Healer") can provide a health boost when you run out of food.
- [1.x/Questers](https://wiki.citizensnpcs.co/1.x/Quester "1.x/Quester") are perhaps the most powerful, featuring a robust questing system.
- [1.x/Traders](https://wiki.citizensnpcs.co/1.x/Trader "1.x/Trader") provide a unique way for your players to buy and sell goods.
- [1.x/Wizards](https://wiki.citizensnpcs.co/1.x/Wizard "1.x/Wizard") can do magical tasks.

Mob Types (These types can automatically spawn):

- [1.x/Evil NPCs](https://wiki.citizensnpcs.co/1.x/Evil_NPCs "1.x/Evil NPCs") can spawn alongside your servers' other mobs.
