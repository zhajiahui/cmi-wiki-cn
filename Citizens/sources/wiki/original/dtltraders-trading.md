## Using the trader

1. With an empty hand right click the NPC
2. To toggle between buying/selling click the lime/cyan wool in the lower right corner.

## Buy menu

1. Clicking once on an item shows the price or brings up the amount selection menu.
2. Clicking the item a second time buys it.

When in the amount selection menu you can click the red wool to go back to the trader's main inventory.

## Sell menu

1. Clicking once on an item shows how much you will get for it.
2. Clicking the item a second time sells it.

**Note:** NPCs will not buy anything unless you set up the accepted items and their prices.
