# Bonemeal {#bonemeal}

## 说明 {#description}

对目标块应用骨粉效果。

## 属性 {#attributes}

| 参数 | 别名 | 说明 | 默认值 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| blockFace | bf, face, f | 要施加骨粉的[方块面](https://hub.spigotmc.org/javadocs/spigot/org/bukkit/block/BlockFace.html)。 | UP |

## 示例 {#examples}

```yaml
  Skills:
  - bonemeal @selflocation
```
