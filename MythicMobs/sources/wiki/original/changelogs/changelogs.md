# 5.13.1 {#5131}

## General {#general}

- Added `PackOverrides` and `FileOverrides` to `packinfo.yml`, the inverse of `PackDependencies` and `FileDependencies`: a pack, or some of its files, is skipped when the listed pack or plugin IS present. All four keys now accept conditions combined with `&&`, `||`, `!` and parentheses, and a name can be a plugin, another pack's folder name, or `MythicMobsPremium`

```yaml
PackDependencies:
- ((MythicCrucible && MythicMobsPremium) || Nexo)
PackOverrides:
- TheRealPack
FileDependencies:
- armors/** MythicArmors
FileOverrides:
- items/armors_vanilla.yml MythicArmors
```

- Fixed `FileDependencies` never excluding files on a real server

## Conditions {#conditions}

- `inBounds` accepts the new shapes, location targeters, and corner overrides listed under Triggers
- Fixed `heightAbove`/`heightBelow` not resolving caster and skill placeholders in `height` when the targets are locations

## Targeters {#targeters}

### NEW: bound {#new-bound}

- Added the `@bound` targeter (alias `@bounds`), targeting the living entities inside a cuboid, sphere, or cylinder, with the same options as `inBounds`

```yaml
- ignite @bound{shape=sphere;l=@selfLocation;r=8}
```

## Triggers {#triggers}

### NEW: onPlayerEnter / onPlayerExit {#new-onplayerenter-onplayerexit}

- Added mob triggers that fire once for each player who moves into or out of a region, checked on the mob's timer clock; `@trigger` is the player. Leaving by logging out, dying, or changing worlds does not fire `onPlayerExit`

```yaml
Skills:
- message{m=Turn back while you can} @trigger ~onPlayerEnter{shape=sphere;location=@selfLocation;radius=20}
```

### Bounds Shapes {#bounds-shapes}

- `onEnterBounds`, `onExitBounds`, `onPlayerEnter`, `onPlayerExit`, `inBounds`, and `@bound` accept `shape=sphere` (with `location` and `radius`) and `shape=cylinder` (also `height`), besides the default two-corner cuboid
- Corners and locations accept a location targeter such as `@selfLocation` or `@CasterSpawnLocation`, resolved on every check
- Added `x1`/`y1`/`z1` and `x2`/`y2`/`z2`, replacing one coordinate of a resolved corner, e.g. for a region between two pins at any height
- Bounds trigger config errors now say what is wrong, e.g. a cylinder without `height`

```yaml
Skills:
- teleport @CasterSpawnLocation ~onExitBounds{shape=sphere;location=@CasterSpawnLocation;radius=20}
- message{m=Welcome to the arena} @trigger ~onPlayerEnter{c1=arena_a;c2=arena_b;y1=-64;y2=320}
```
