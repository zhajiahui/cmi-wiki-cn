# Random Look Activity

The `Random Look Activity` is used to make the entity look in random directions.

## How could this be used? {#how-could-this-be-used}

This could be used to make the entity look distracted.

## Fields {#fields}

## Pitch Range

Required

## Yaw Range

Required

## Duration

Required

The duration between each look

Durations can be specified in the following format: `1d 2h 3m 4s`. The following units are supported: `d` for days, `h` for hours,`m` for minutes and `s` for seconds.
