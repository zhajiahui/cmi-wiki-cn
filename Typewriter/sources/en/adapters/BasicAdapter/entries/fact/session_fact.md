# Session Fact

This [fact](https://docs.typewritermc.com/docs/creating-stories/facts) is stored until the player logs out.

## How could this be used? {#how-could-this-be-used}

This could be used to slowly add up a player's total time played, and reward them with a badge or other reward when they reach a certain amount of time.
