# Cron Fact

A [fact](https://docs.typewritermc.com/docs/creating-stories/facts) that is saved until a specified date, like (0 0 * * 1).

## How could this be used? {#how-could-this-be-used}

This fact could be used to create weekly rewards, which are reset every week. Or to simulate the opening hours of a shop.

## Fields {#fields}

## Comment

Inherited Optional

A comment to keep track of what this fact is used for.

Required

## Cron

Required
