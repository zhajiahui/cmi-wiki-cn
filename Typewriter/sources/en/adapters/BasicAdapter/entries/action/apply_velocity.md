# Apply Velocity Action

The `ApplyVelocityActionEntry` is an action that applies a velocity to the player.

## How could this be used? {#how-could-this-be-used}

This action can be useful in a variety of situations. You can use it to simulate wind, explosions, or any other force that would move the player.

## Fields {#fields}

## Criteria

Inherited List

A list of facts that must be met by the player before this entry can be triggered.

## Modifiers

Inherited List

A list of facts that will be modified for the player when this entry is triggered.

## Triggers

Required List

## Force

Required

The force to apply to the player.
