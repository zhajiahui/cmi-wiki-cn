# Player Join Event

The `Player Join Event` event is called when a player joins the server.

## How could this be used? {#how-could-this-be-used}

This could be used with [facts](https://docs.typewritermc.com/docs/creating-stories/facts) to give a new player a welcome message, or welcome back new players. You can also use it to give new players a starting item, or to give them a starting amount of money with the [Vault adapter](https://docs.typewritermc.com/adapters/VaultAdapter).
