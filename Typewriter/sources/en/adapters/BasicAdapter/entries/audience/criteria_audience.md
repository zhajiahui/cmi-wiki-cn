# Criteria Audience

The `Criteria Audience` entry filters an audience based on criteria.

## How could this be used? {#how-could-this-be-used}

This could be used to show a boss bar or npc when the player is in a certain quest stage.

## Fields {#fields}

## Children

Required List

## Criteria

Inherited List

A list of facts that must be met by the player before this entry can be triggered.

## Inverted

Required
