# Trigger Audience

The `Trigger Audience` entry is an audience filter that triggers a sequence when the player enters or exits the audience.

## How could this be used? {#how-could-this-be-used}

This can be used to bridge the gap between audiences and sequence pages.

## Fields {#fields}

## On Enter

Required

The sequence to trigger when the player enters the audience.

## On Exit

Required

The sequence to trigger when the player exits the audience.
